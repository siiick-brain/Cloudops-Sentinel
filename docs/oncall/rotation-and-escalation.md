# On-call rotation and escalation

The primary on-call owns acknowledgement and initial triage. The secondary on-call supports investigation and takes over when the primary is unavailable. Escalate an unresolved SEV1 to the incident lead and service owner. For SEV2, page the service owner when the mitigation is unclear or SLO burn continues.

Set rotation length, paging hours and contact methods in the team's approved scheduling system. This repository does not contain a live roster.
